Awinlink Community Guidelines

Help people showcase talent, learn and find opportunities safely. These guidelines apply to profiles, posts, media, messages, calls, classrooms, recruitment and the talent market.

Respect people

No harassment, threats, hateful abuse, discriminatory targeting, sexual exploitation, or sharing private information without permission. Respect consent and boundaries in messages and calls. Do not share child sexual abuse material or exploit minors.

Be honest and protect others

No impersonation, fake qualifications, misleading opportunities, fraud, phishing, spam, coercion or requests for account credentials. Clearly describe offers and any costs. Do not pressure people to share identity documents or money. Verify opportunities independently.

Share responsibly

Only post content you have permission to use. Respect intellectual property, confidentiality, audience settings and recording consent. Do not distribute malicious files, illegal material or instructions intended to harm others. Do not evade bans or interfere with service security.

Report and block

Use Report on a profile, post or message and block unwanted contacts using the app's safety controls. For other issues or to request review of a moderation action, contact support with relevant context. Do not include passwords or authentication tokens. For an immediate threat, contact appropriate local emergency services; Awinlink is not an emergency service.

Enforcement

Reports may be reviewed and may lead to content removal, feature restrictions or account suspension. Context, seriousness and repeated behavior can affect the response. Reporting does not guarantee a particular result. The operator must finalize review, appeal, notice and child-safety escalation procedures before release.
