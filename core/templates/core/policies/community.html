Awinlink Community Guidelines

These standards apply to profiles, posts, media, messages, calls, classrooms, recruitment and the talent market. Operator approval and operational procedures are still pending.

Child safety: abuse and exploitation are prohibited

Awinlink prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, sextortion and trafficking for sexual exploitation. Do not create, upload, request, distribute or link to sexual content involving minors, including generated depictions. Do not pressure minors for private images, personal locations, identity documents, money or off-platform contact.

Do not bypass age or guardian restrictions, use existing connections to evade contact approval, or misrepresent your age or relationship to a child. Guardian permission never authorizes exploitation or other prohibited conduct.

Report a profile, post or message using in-app Report, and block unwanted contacts. For classroom/call incidents or other material without a report control, use support and child-safety contact details. Provide usernames, approximate time and relevant record links; do not download, forward or attach suspected CSAM. For immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services. Awinlink is not an emergency service.

Respect people

No harassment, threats, hateful abuse, discriminatory targeting, sexual exploitation, stalking or disclosure of private information without permission. Respect consent and boundaries in messages and calls. Do not share addresses, school locations or personal contact details of minors.

Be honest and share responsibly

No impersonation, fake qualifications, misleading opportunities, fraud, phishing, spam, coercion or requests for credentials. Verify opportunities independently. Share only content you have permission to use; respect confidentiality, audience settings and recording consent. Do not distribute malicious files, illegal material or instructions intended to harm others. Do not evade bans or interfere with security.

Reporting, enforcement and review

Reports can lead to review, content removal, feature restrictions or suspension. Seriousness, context and repeated behavior may affect decisions. Reporting does not guarantee a particular outcome. Contact support to request review of an action; never send passwords or authentication tokens.

Before release, the operator must confirm a staffed reporting process, handling/removal of known CSAM, preservation and reporting obligations under applicable law, appeals and notices, and a designated child-safety point of contact in Play Console where required. These published draft standards are not evidence that those operational or legal requirements have been completed.
