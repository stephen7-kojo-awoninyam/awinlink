Information processed

We process account details including username, name, email, password credentials, phone and country; profiles including category and specific talent; uploaded media, portfolio and learning content; posts, messages and calls-related metadata; applications, invitations, team membership, offers and deal messages; and blocking and moderation reports. We record the Terms version and acceptance time when a new user agrees. Existing users without a record are not marked as having consented.

Service requests can generate technical and security records. Camera, microphone and screen sharing are used when you choose relevant features and grant device permissions. Push notification tokens and device information enable alerts and calls. Browser sessions and authentication tokens support sign-in and security.

Why information is used

Information supports account access, profiles, search, recommendations, messaging, learning, recruitment, team administration, service safety and troubleshooting. Recommendations can use profile details, interests and interactions. A recommendation is not an employment or eligibility decision. Terms acceptance is separate from optional marketing or device permissions.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google processes your authentication under its own policies and sends Awinlink a signed identity response containing a stable account identifier, verified email and basic name information. Awinlink keeps the Google account identifier to recognize your account; matching email addresses alone do not link accounts. Linking an existing password account requires explicit confirmation and authentication. Temporary sign-in records expire after ten minutes and are removed by scheduled cleanup. Google access and refresh tokens are not stored. Existing accounts without Terms acceptance records remain unmarked.

Sharing and visibility

Profiles and content are shared according to audience and access controls. Messages, applications and offers are accessible to their authorized participants; authorized service personnel may process information for support and safety. Recipients can retain information they receive. Hosting and media storage providers process service data. Expo, Apple APNs and Google FCM support notifications. WebRTC calls can connect peers and configured relay servers. Messages are stored by the service and should not be assumed to be end-to-end encrypted.

Your choices

Edit your profile, use available privacy and recommendation controls, block users, report abuse and change device permissions. Request access, correction, export or deletion through support; available rights depend on applicable law. You can delete your account in a browser or through Account and safety in the app. Organization owners must transfer ownership first.

Retention and security

Account deletion removes the account and related database content according to service relationships. Moderation reports may remain with the reporter account link removed. Uploaded files and backups can persist separately. No system provides absolute security; protect your credentials and avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

Items requiring legal and operational review

Confirm the complete data and vendor inventory, analytics and advertising practices, cookies and similar storage, processing purposes and legal bases, recipients, international transfers and safeguards, retention schedules and backup/media cleanup, request and complaint channels, children's data and age rules, incident procedures and change notices before release. This draft does not assert unverified retention periods, data-sale practices or compliance certifications.
