Information processed and purposes

  • Account and authentication: username, name, email, phone, country, account role, password hashes for password accounts, Google identity links, sessions and access/refresh tokens. These support registration, security, recovery and access. Google-only accounts do not require an Awinlink password.
  • Age and guardian records: date of birth and its source, nominated guardian email, guardian and reviewer account references, verification time and case reference, consent version/time, approvals, revocations, audit events and individual contact/course permissions. These support age restrictions and guardian authorization. Birth date and guardian records are not public profile fields.
  • Profiles and community activity: talent categories, skills, interests, biographies, profile locations, portfolio work, posts, comments, likes, follows, saved items, recommendations and interactions. These support publication, discovery and ranking.
  • Communication: message text, files, images, video and voice messages, conversation membership, call identifiers, participants, status and timing. These support messaging and calls.
  • Learning and opportunities: course content, submissions, quizzes and attempts, progress, certificates, classroom invitations, attendance, classroom chat/whiteboard activity, applications, recruitment, organization teams, offers and deal messages. These support learning and collaboration; access depends on role and permission.
  • Safety and technical data: blocks, reports and evidence, notification subscriptions, push/VoIP tokens and device platform, service/security records and network information associated with requests. Authentication abuse controls use hashed identifiers; service providers may process IP addresses. We record the Terms version and acceptance time, separately from guardian consent.

Children, teenagers and guardian authorization

The implemented account bands are under 13 (guardian-managed), 13–17 (protected teen), and 18 or older (adult). These technical bands do not establish a legal minimum age in every country; launch eligibility and applicable parental-consent requirements remain subject to approval. Existing accounts with no recorded birth date remain unknown-age accounts, not verified adults. Age declarations are self-reported.

Under-13 registration stores the submitted registration details, birth date and nominated guardian email before approval, but the account stays inactive and receives no signed-in session or mobile credentials. A guardian nomination or Google login alone is not guardian approval. An independent authorized staff reviewer must verify the adult guardian and relationship, followed by explicit approval by that guardian. Verification is a manual process; this draft does not certify that it meets every legal requirement for verifiable parental consent.

Guardians can manage approved contacts, separate call permission, course access and separate live-classroom participation, and view scoped learning progress. Guardian authority does not automatically permit reading private messages or quiz answers or grant instructor/organization powers. Ordinary adult or unknown-age accounts cannot initiate unsolicited contact with known minors. Known minors are excluded from public discovery; structured personal names and location fields are restricted for other viewers, with authorized account/guardian access retained.

Withdrawal of guardian authorization restricts the affected minor account and denies further protected access. Server checks apply immediately; stopping media on a disconnected device depends on control delivery or watchdogs. Withdrawal is not account deletion. Guardian authority ends at adulthood, but account correction, birthday handover and recovery can require support. Deleting a guardian can restrict the child account; it does not delete the child.

Do not put addresses, school details or other sensitive information in usernames, free text, files or images. Automatic removal of personal information from free text or embedded photo/video metadata is not implemented. Historical public media URLs and copies already obtained by recipients need separate handling. Guardians may request review, correction or deletion through the privacy contact; authority must be checked, and this is not an automatic guardian deletion/export tool.

Google sign-in and deletion confirmation

Choosing Google sign-in sends authentication requests to Google and provides Awinlink a signed response with a stable Google account identifier, verified email and basic name information. Google processes its service under its own policies. Awinlink stores the linked account identifier; email matches alone never link accounts. Existing password accounts require explicit authenticated linking. Google access/refresh tokens are not stored by this integration.

Temporary sign-in/linking challenges expire after ten minutes. Separate deletion challenges expire after five minutes and require a recent signed Google authentication time and the already-linked Google identity. These are authorization deadlines, not promises that database rows are physically erased at those times. Successful exchanges clear temporary verifier/nonce material; successful completion clears temporary identity claims. A cleanup command removes expired attempts, but its production schedule and completion must be verified.

Device permissions, storage and calls

Camera, microphone, screen sharing and selected file/photo access support the features you choose. You can deny or withdraw device permissions; affected features may stop working. Native credentials use device secure storage; browser authentication uses session storage, session cookies and CSRF protection. Notification subscriptions and tokens support notifications, including incoming calls; alert previews can be visible on a device's lock screen.

WebRTC calls and classrooms connect participants directly or through configured TURN relays. Direct peers can learn network addresses. Relay-only routing is configuration-dependent and is not guaranteed by this draft. Voice messages and uploaded recordings are stored content. Transport encryption does not mean stored messages or files are end-to-end encrypted.

Visibility and service providers

Authorized recipients, course/classroom staff and organization members can see information permitted by audience, membership and role controls. Authorized service personnel may process data for operation, safety and support. Recipients can keep copies outside Awinlink's control. External links take you to services with their own practices.

The implementation supports the following providers; the operator must confirm which are active, their legal identities, countries, contracts and retention before release:

  • Hosting, database, cache and backup operators process service records. Railway hosting is referenced in release configuration; VPS hosting is also supported. The active deployment and backup providers require confirmation.
  • Cloudinary media storage or configured filesystem storage processes uploads. Historical public CDN URLs require separate privatization/invalidation checks.
  • Google handles optional authentication. Expo, Google FCM, Apple APNs and browser push services can process notification tokens, routing metadata and alert payloads for enabled platforms.
  • Configured SMTP providers or Resend can process recovery email recipients and messages. TURN operators handle relayed call traffic and network metadata.
  • Paystack integration exists for web advertising payments and can receive payer email, amounts, currency and payment references when that feature is enabled. This is not a claim that payments or native purchases are enabled at launch.

Recommendations and advertising

Recommendations can use profiles, interests and interactions and are not employment or eligibility decisions. The web service supports sponsored campaigns and records impressions, clicks and video views using account-linked tracking identifiers. Ad selection can use talent category, domains, skills and sports, with frequency caps. Ad delivery and tracking are restricted to active accounts with a recorded adult age; minors and unknown-age accounts are excluded. This does not certify any third-party SDK for child-directed use.

The operator must verify enabled analytics, SDK data flows, advertiser recipients and any data-sale/sharing or marketing practices. This draft makes no unverified “no ads,” “no tracking,” or “we never sell data” claim. Mobile store disclosures must reflect the released build and backend.

Retention and account deletion

You can start deletion in Account and safety in the app or at the browser deletion page without reinstalling the app. Password accounts can confirm their current password; linked Google accounts can use fresh Google confirmation without an Awinlink password. Explicit confirmation is required. Organization owners must transfer ownership first; protected shared learning references can require support resolution. Restricted/inactive accounts may need a verified support process rather than an authentication bypass.

  • Successful account deletion removes the account and related database content according to its relationships, including the linked Google identity and owned posts/messages. It ends account access; guardian revocation alone does not erase data.
  • Some shared classroom chat, whiteboard, audit, advertising-event and moderation records can remain with the user relation removed. Removing an account link does not guarantee that free text or evidence is anonymous. Approved purposes, erasure procedures and maximum retention periods for these records remain to be confirmed.
  • Uploaded files are not all automatically erased when database records are deleted. Historical CDN URLs, cached/distributed copies and backups require separate cleanup. Backup expiration, restoration safeguards and storage deletion completion are not verified here.
  • Account/guardian records, logs, recovery records and other service data need operator-approved retention schedules. Challenge/token expiry is not the retention schedule for these records. No unverified deletion deadline or legal retention exemption is promised.

Requests, security and changes

Use the privacy contact or support to request access, correction, export, deletion or review of a privacy concern. These are request channels, not a claim that every request has an automated tool. Identity and guardian authority must be checked. Applicable rights, response times, lawful processing grounds, complaint authorities and international-transfer safeguards require confirmation for launch countries. Protect your credentials; no system guarantees absolute security.

The operator must approve policy-change notices and any renewed consent/acceptance before release. Existing accounts are not retrospectively marked as having accepted a new Terms version. Retention, vendor and legal details remain release blockers until verified and approved.
