Awinlink support

For sign-in problems, use password recovery. You can delete your account here without reinstalling the app.

Google-only accounts should sign in with Google. They can confirm account deletion with their linked Google identity without setting an Awinlink password. Password reset applies to eligible password accounts.

For abuse, use Report on the relevant profile, post or message. Block accounts under Account and safety to manage unwanted contact.

For child-safety concerns, submit a text-only safety report without signing in, or use Report a child-safety concern in the app's Account and safety screen. The form stores reports for administrator review; monitoring and response procedures still need operator confirmation.

Delete your Awinlink account

Successful deletion removes your account and related database content according to its relationships, including your linked Google identity, profile and owned posts/messages. Shared classroom chat, whiteboard, audit, advertising-event and moderation records can remain with your user link removed; their text is not automatically anonymous. Uploaded files, historical public CDN URLs, recipient copies and backups need separate cleanup. Complete physical erasure and retention schedules are not yet verified. See the retention details or contact privacy support.

Organization owners must transfer ownership first in the organization's team settings. Protected shared learning references can require support resolution. Guardian withdrawal is not deletion; deleting a guardian does not delete their child. Restricted/inactive accounts require verified support handling rather than bypassing guardian safeguards.
