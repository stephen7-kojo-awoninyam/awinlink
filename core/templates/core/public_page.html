Awinlink privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026.

Awinlink connects talent, organizations, scouts and coaches. {% if operator %}The service operator is {{ operator }}.{% endif %}

Information used by the service

We process account details such as your username, email and password credentials, profile information you supply, community posts, portfolio and learning content, applications, messages, uploaded files, and safety reports. Some profile fields, including phone and country, depend on the information you provide. Your chosen audience and the service's visibility rules determine who can access community content.

Camera, microphone and screen sharing are used when you choose calling, voice messaging or classroom features. Device permissions can be changed in your device settings. Notification tokens and device platform information enable alerts and incoming calls.

Purpose and service providers

Information supports authentication, community discovery and recommendations, messaging, learning, team administration, security and moderation. Hosting and storage services process service data. Expo, Apple APNs and Google FCM process push delivery information. Calls use WebRTC peers and configured relay servers. Messages are stored by the service; do not assume they are end-to-end encrypted.

Deletion and retention

You can delete your account using a browser or use Account and safety in the app. Organization owners must transfer ownership first. Account deletion removes your account and related database content. Moderation reports can remain with the reporter account link removed. Uploaded objects and backups may persist separately; storage cleanup and backup retention periods must be confirmed by the operator before release.

Your choices and questions

Edit your profile, control device permissions, block accounts, report content, or request account deletion. For access, correction, retention or privacy questions, use Awinlink support.

Use accurate account information and protect your password. Only share material you have permission to use. You retain rights to your content and permit Awinlink to store, process and display it to deliver the service according to visibility settings. Report abusive content or accounts in the app and block unwanted contacts. Moderators may review reports and restrict accounts or remove content.

The mobile launch is free and purchases are unavailable. Availability of opportunities and user content does not guarantee employment, selection or results. You can end your use and delete your account. Contact support for questions.
